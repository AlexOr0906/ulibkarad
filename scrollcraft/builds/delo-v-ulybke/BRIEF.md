# Дело в улыбке. Scroll Craft pass

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. Existing Astro concept refined, not rebuilt from a template.

## Evidence and eight decisions

1. Vibe. User: «Авторский премиальный минимализм», «серебро/металлик, белый, графит», «спокойный темп, высокая читаемость». Supplied silver brandboard and family-in-tooth mark anchor the result.
2. Journey. User required Hero, services, approach, doctors, appointment CTA, contacts, footer. Preserve all seven, with ordinary anchor navigation.
3. Energy. Quiet opening and service index, one composed moment inside approach, plain team placeholders, a clear demo appointment and practical contacts.
4. Feel. Recognition, orientation, attention, honesty, readiness, certainty. The one memorable moment is the family mark completing alongside the three principles.
5. Signature. Authored decision: the supplied family mark is gradually engraved in silver as the approach principles pass. Whole mark is always available in a subdued base; nothing essential waits for animation.
6. Range. User explicitly asked premium-minimal. No neon, blobs, toy 3D or cinematic spectacle.
7. World. Separate sections in normal flow. No unbroken flight, scroll hijacking, video, artificial blank screens or full-screen pinned scenes.
8. Assets. Supplied brandboard plus family logo. The second mark was vectorized, preserving silhouette. No genuine clinic, doctor or patient photography supplied, so none invented.

## Purpose and next action

Audience: prospective patients evaluating the concept of a private dental clinic in Magnitogorsk. Intended belief: the direction feels clear, calm and attentive. Primary navigation/CTA label: «Обсудить визит». Form is a local demonstration, not a medical appointment.

- Services: orientation, from a calm plain index without illustration noise.

Peak: «Пока я читал о подходе, семейный знак постепенно стал цельным» in approach. It gets the only sticky composition and the longest authored desktop narrative within a section. There is no authored empty scroll; the preceding services are quiet information.

Tell-someone sentence: «Это сайт, где семейный знак собирается в серебристую подпись рядом с принципами заботы».

## Grammar and gate

Custom grammar: quiet clinical editorial. Persistent practical anchor navigation, split title and supplied sign, service index, one local sticky graphic paired with readable principles, honest team placeholders, demo interaction, compact contacts and a closing invitation.

Constraints: normal vertical reading, no media behind text, no invented clinical proof, no video, no rails, no magnet/tilt, no scene crossfades, no extra scroll spacer. Mobile uses one static mark and stacked prose. Motion-off shows the entire mark and all text.

Why the eight standard grammars did not fit literally: filmic one-shot and continuous world oppose practical jumps and calm flow; chaptered editorial forbids the requested persistent header and logo opening; live surface misrepresents a clinic as software; typographic poster forbids useful service/doctor grouping and undersells supplied identity; gallery turns care into an object catalogue; split stage implies an unsupported before/after argument; cutlist is too fast. The custom grammar states actual constraints rather than relabeling a filmic template.
